


Our contributions are threefold.
(i) We define a representation of distributed multipartite quantum states with the necessary abstractions for the efficient analysis of quantum network protocols.
(ii) We define the semantics of an algebraic language using said abstractions to describe general multipartite entanglement distribution protocols.
(iii) We propose an efficient method for the analysis of protocols expressed in our language. Our aim is to evaluate these protocols in terms of success probability \ale{time to success?} and final (distributed) state fidelity.
