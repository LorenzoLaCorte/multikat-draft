Multipartite distribution motivates an algebraic language whose resources capture both entanglement structure and network location. We have identified the corresponding design questions and outlined graph-state classes, explicit action contracts, and composition inspired by BellKAT/PBKAT. The immediate goals are to connect these contracts to physical implementations and prove soundness of protocol composition. A subsequent tool could check protocol equivalence and quantify delivery success and resource use. Extending the resource domain to describe retained states after loss, recovery procedures, noise, and finite hardware capacity will be necessary to cover the all-photonic protocols that motivate this work.
